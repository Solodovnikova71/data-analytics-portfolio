# Privacy and governance assessment

**DAT 250 | Academic coursework | Private review draft**

An academic assessment of fictional AmityTech Solutions after missed audits, falsified audit records and storage-device control violations. The paper connects privacy concepts, ethical judgment and operational accountability.

[Read the one-page PDF](case-study.pdf)

## Scenario

The fictional company provides technology services in the United States and Ireland and also produces anonymized consumer profiles. The assignment describes possible exposure of client accounts and asks how leadership should respond.

## Analysis documented

- Compared GDPR and CCPA concepts as part of the coursework, including transparency, accountability and consumer privacy.
- Examined audit integrity, privacy governance, cross-border data handling and incident-response decision making.
- Considered ethical questions around consumer profiling, confidentiality and trust, alongside legal compliance.
- Mapped the roles of leadership, auditors, compliance, cybersecurity, governance and client-facing teams.

## Recommendations in the paper

Strengthen privacy and security policies; independently review audit completion and documentation; clarify responsibilities and separation of duties; reinforce access and device controls; train staff on escalation; and prepare a transparent response strategy with appropriate legal review.

## Why the analysis matters

The assessment treats failed audits and dishonest documentation as management and accountability problems. It explains why a response should address the way decisions and controls are overseen, alongside technical safeguards.

## Scope and evidence

This is a coursework policy analysis of a fictional organization. It is not a real incident investigation, implemented compliance program, legal opinion or statement of current legal requirements. The recommendations were not shown to have been implemented or to have produced measured outcomes.

## Skills demonstrated

Policy analysis; privacy risk assessment; governance; ethical decision making.
